How it works

From sign-up to release day

RRR is a community label. You pay a small monthly membership, book releases for a one-off upload fee, and keep 100% of your royalties.

A

For artists

  1. Create your account

    Join as an artist for €1.00 a month. You get community membership, promotion opportunities and a free personalised Community Card. Create account

  2. Link your Bandcamp

    Link your Bandcamp account to the RRR catalogue. While your releases stay active in the catalogue you get free RRR VIP Membership.

  3. Book your release

    Pick a release date in the RRR release calendar and choose Bandcamp, streaming or both. Upload fees: single €1.00, EP €2.00, album €2.50. Book a release

  4. Send your assets on time

    Put your audio, artwork and release details in your designated Google Drive folder at least 3 weeks before release for Bandcamp, and at least 2 weeks before for streaming. Bandcamp editorial is separate from RRR: if you want to try for it, submit it yourself at least 8–9 weeks before release.

  5. Release day

    Your release goes live on your Bandcamp page and the RRR page, and on streaming through the RRR RouteNote account. Bandcamp payments go straight to your own account.

  6. Promote it with the community

    Book a promotional post on RRR social media, upload a video to the RRR YouTube channel, and submit to The Bandcamp Hour through Groover. See booking options

B

For fans

  1. Join as a fan

    Fan membership is €0.50 a month and includes your own personalised Community Card. Create account

  2. Get close to the artists

    Talk directly with RRR artists and take part in community events and activities.

  3. Support and earn

    Buy music, share releases and help other members to earn community points and a shot at Fan of the Month.

Common questions

Do I keep my royalties?

Yes. Artists keep 100% of royalties from Bandcamp and streaming platforms, and keep ownership of their music and intellectual property.

What if I need a break?

Switch to Community Supporter Status: €0.50 a month for artists, €0.25 for fans. You keep your membership, rank, points and achievements, but can't book releases or promotions until you reactivate. You can reactivate at any time.

Can RRR run offers on my release?

Yes. Releases in the RRR Bandcamp catalogue can be included in RRR discount codes, giveaways and promotional offers. This is a condition of the Bandcamp service.

How do I pay?

Membership is paid monthly through PayPal, which also takes debit and credit cards. Payment opens when sign-up launches.
