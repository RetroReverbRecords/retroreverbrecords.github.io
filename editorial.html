Editorial guides

How to pitch your release to Bandcamp and Spotify editors yourself, following the platforms' own instructions.

A

Bandcamp editorial

Bandcamp Daily, Album of the Day, New & Notable and more.

How to submit

  1. Timing

    Bandcamp: "Eight weeks before your release date is ideal." We recommend 8–9 weeks. Less than 4–5 weeks rarely works.

  2. Set up the release on Bandcamp

    Saved as a draft or private release. Bandcamp only accepts Bandcamp links, not SoundCloud, Dropbox or Spotify.

  3. Tell the story

    Write the story behind the record in "about this album" and in your pitch. Don't just send a press release.

  4. Fill in Bandcamp's form

    It goes straight to an editor. Bandcamp editorial submission form ↗

Improve your chances

  • Albums are covered more than singles.
  • Set up a pre-order with at least one track streamable or downloadable.
  • Custom header and background, artist photo and bio, merch linked to the release.
  • Bandcamp gets about 1,000 pitches a week and can't cover them all. Don't take a no personally.

B

Spotify editorial

Pitching to Spotify's playlist editors through Spotify for Artists.

How to pitch

  1. Deliver early

    Your release must be delivered and showing as upcoming in Spotify for Artists. Our 3-week streaming deadline gives you time for that.

  2. Open the pitch form

    Log in at artists.spotify.com → Music → Upcoming → pick your song → pitch.

  3. At least 7 days before release

    Spotify: pitch an unreleased song at least 7 days ahead and it goes into your followers' Release Radar. Earlier is better.

  4. Give detail

    Fill in genre, mood, instruments and the story. Spotify says more detail gives your song a better chance.

Rules to know

  • One song at a time. Once it's live you can pitch the next.
  • You can't pitch compilations or songs you're only featured on.
  • Anyone on your team with Admin or Editor access can pitch.
  • Pitching doesn't guarantee a placement.

C

Add RRR to your Spotify for Artists team

Optional. Add RRR as your label and we can help: checking your upcoming releases, preparing pitches with you, and keeping your profile in shape.

  1. Open your team

    In Spotify for Artists, open your profile menu and go to Team.

  2. Invite RRR

    Invite retroreverbrecords@gmail.com, with role Editor so we can help with pitches, or Reader if you only want us to see stats.

  3. Tell us

    Email us when you've sent the invite so we can accept it.

You stay in charge: you can change RRR's role or remove it at any time.
