AI check · dispute

Flagged by mistake?

Our robot checks every release for signs of AI generation. Robots get it wrong sometimes. If yours was flagged and you made it yourself, send us proof here. A person reviews every dispute within 7 days.

A

What counts as proof

Read this before you send anything. The best proof shows the song being made, with dates, in files an AI tool doesn't produce.

Strong proof

  • Your project session: a screenshot or, better, a short screen recording of the DAW project (Ableton, FL Studio, Logic…) with all the tracks, plugins and the song's arrangement.
  • Early versions with dates: demos, voice memos, rough mixes or bounce files saved before the final.
  • A video of you playing or singing a part of the song.
  • MIDI files and dry recordings (vocals or instruments before effects).
  • Collaborators: names and contacts of anyone who played, sang, mixed or mastered it.
  • Licences for samples, loops or sound packs you used.

Doesn't help much

  • Only the finished master file again
  • A screenshot from another AI detector saying "human" (detectors disagree with each other)
  • "It's on Spotify already", or likes and comments
  • Lyrics alone, or cover artwork
  • Stems on their own: some AI tools can make stems too, so add a session screenshot or early versions
  • A long explanation with nothing to back it up

Good to know

  • Tell us about any AI tools you did use, such as AI mastering, AI stem separation or AI vocal tuning. They can trip the check, and using them doesn't make a song "AI-generated".
  • Big files (project folders, stems, videos): share a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link, set to "anyone with the link".
  • Your proof is only used to review this case, and deleted after 90 days.
  • If we can't clear it, you can still release on streaming, or withdraw the booking and get the upload fee back if we haven't uploaded it yet.
