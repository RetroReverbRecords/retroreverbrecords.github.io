Who we are

Retro Reverb Records ("RRR") runs this website and the RRR Community. For anything about your data, email retroreverbrecords@gmail.com .

Where it's kept and who sees it

  • Member records are stored in RRR's Google account (Google Sheets, Gmail and Calendar). Payments go through PayPal. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps basic visit logs (like IP addresses) for security.
  • Your member dashboard shows your name, rank, points, achievements, releases, posts and stats to anyone with your member ID. It never shows your email, address or payment details. Ask us to make it private at any time.
  • We don't sell your data. We only share it with the services above so they can do their job, or if the law requires it. Google, PayPal and GitHub may process data in the USA; they use the safeguards EU law requires (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).
  • Players from Spotify and Groover only load if you tap to play them. They then set their own cookies.
  • Your browser keeps small notes for this site (your cookie choice, member ID in the app, beta mode). They stay on your device.

How long we keep it

  • Member details: while you're a member, then up to 12 months so you can come back.
  • Payment and signature records: as long as tax and contract law requires (up to 10 years in Italy, 6 in the UK).
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Safety records: 12 months for warnings; for removals, as long as the block lasts.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the ICO in the UK or the Garante in Italy.
