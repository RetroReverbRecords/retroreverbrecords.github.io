How to get your synthwave track on radio and playlists

Synthwave has a big network of independent radio shows, playlists and blogs. Here's how to reach them, and how to avoid paying for nothing.

1. Get ready before you pitch

  • A finished, mastered track. Curators judge in the first 30 seconds, so put your strongest moment early.
  • A link that plays straight away: Spotify, Bandcamp, SoundCloud or YouTube. Avoid downloads that need a sign-in.
  • Your artwork and a short bio (two or three lines: who you are, your sound, where you're from).
  • Your social handles, so curators can tag you when they play you.

2. Pick the right curators

Ten curators who love your style beat a hundred who don't. Before you send anything:

  • Listen to a few recent episodes or the latest playlist adds. Do they play tracks like yours?
  • Check the genre: synthwave, darksynth, outrun, dreamwave and synthpop audiences are not the same.
  • Read their submission rules and follow them exactly. Many shows ignore pitches that break them.

3. Write a short, personal pitch

Keep it to a few lines. Say who you are, what the track is, why it fits their show or playlist, and include the link. Mention an episode or track of theirs you liked. Don't send attachments unless they ask.

4. Pay for a listen, never for placement

Paid submission services are fine when you're paying for a guaranteed listen and honest feedback. Be careful with anyone who guarantees streams or playlist adds for money: Spotify forbids paying for playlist placement, and fake streams can get your music removed.

5. Follow up and say thank you

  • If you're played, share it and tag the show. Curators remember artists who promote their shows.
  • If you're declined, read the feedback. It's often the most useful thing you'll get all year.
  • Keep a simple list of who played you, so you can send them your next release first.
