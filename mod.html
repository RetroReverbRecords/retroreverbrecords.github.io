How to moderate

  • Follow the Code of Conduct steps: friendly word first, then warning, then suspension (up to 30 days), then removal. Zero-tolerance cases can go straight to removal.
  • Always write a clear reason. The member gets it by email, with how to appeal within 14 days.
  • Every action is logged and Cybertronix is told straight away. Mistakes can be undone by setting the member back to good.
  • Rude posts on Discord: delete them there with your Moderator role, then warn here if needed.
