Guideline · not an RRR service

Bandcamp editorial

Bandcamp's editorial team is independent of RRR, and RRR does not submit releases to it. If you want your release considered, send it yourself at least 8–9 weeks before release day. That is the minimum, so plan it before you book your RRR release.
