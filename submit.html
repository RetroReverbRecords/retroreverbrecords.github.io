How it works

  1. Pick your curators

    Radio shows, playlists and blogs that love synth and electronic music. Each one costs €1.00.

  2. Send your track

    A Bandcamp, SoundCloud, YouTube or private link, plus a short message.

  3. They listen and answer

    Every curator writes at least 20 words of honest feedback and accepts or declines within 7 days.

  4. No answer, no charge

    If a curator doesn't answer in time, you get that part of your payment back.

You pay for a listen and honest feedback, never for placement. Curators decide freely, and Spotify forbids paying for playlist adds.
