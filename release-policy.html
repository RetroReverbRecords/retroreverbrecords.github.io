For artists

Release policy

Everything we need from you for a release, and when. Get this right and your release goes out on time with the best chance of being heard.

A

Timeline

  1. 9 wks

    Bandcamp editorial pitch (you)

    If you want Bandcamp editorial consideration, pitch 8–9 weeks ahead. How

  2. Book

    Book your date

    Book on the Book page. Your date is confirmed when you accept RRR's calendar invite.

  3. 3 wks

    Streaming assets due

    Everything below in your RRR Google Drive folder at least 3 weeks before release for streaming.

  4. 2 wks

    Bandcamp assets due

    At least 2 weeks before release for Bandcamp. We upload a draft you can check before it goes live.

  5. 7 days

    Spotify pitch (you)

    Pitch in Spotify for Artists at least 7 days before release so it reaches your followers' Release Radar. How

  6. Day

    Release day

    It's live. The community gets behind it. See Stronger together.

Late assets may move your release to the next free date.

B

What to send

Put everything in your RRR Google Drive folder. Please don't email files, make your own folders, use other storage, or share files in WhatsApp groups.

Audio

  • WAV files, final masters
  • MP3 files with metadata
  • ISRC codes for each track and an EAN/UPC for the release, if you have them

Artwork

  • Square cover: 1400 × 1400 px, JPG or PNG, for Spotify, Bandcamp and posts
  • Vertical: 9:16, at least 1080 × 1920 px, for Stories, Reels, Shorts, TikTok
  • Original or cleared for commercial use

Video (optional)

  • Vertical video 9:16 MP4 for Stories, Reels and Shorts
  • Horizontal video 16:9 MP4 for YouTube

Words

  • Release description and a line about each song, the story behind it
  • Accurate credits: writers, producers, featured artists, mix and master
  • Lyrics
  • AI use declared for this release (see the AI policy)

You, the artist

  • Up-to-date EPK (press kit): bio, photos, links
  • One link for all your socials, such as Linktree
  • Your Bandcamp linked to the RRR label
  • Optional: RRR added to your Spotify for Artists team

Collaborators

  • Everyone on the release has agreed to it
  • Any money split between you and collaborators is agreed between you
  • Tell us about features, remixes and guest vocals when you book
C

Make your Bandcamp page work

Bandcamp says it features music that shows how best to use Bandcamp. Before release:

  • Custom header and background image on your page
  • A detailed "about this album" with the story of the release
  • Artist photo and bio filled in
  • A pre-order with at least one track streamable
  • Merch linked to the release, if you have any
  • Tags that describe your music and location
D

Promotion

Releases that get pushed get heard. What works:

  • Post at least 3 times a week for 3 weeks before and 3 weeks after release
  • Mostly vertical 9:16 video: reels, stories, shorts
  • Tag RRR in every release post
  • Share your media with RRR so we can post it too
  • Send a Bandcamp message to your fans on release day, from your Bandcamp page, linking the new release
  • 1–2 good posts a week between releases to keep your audience warm
  • Support other RRR releases: it earns points and it's how we all grow (why)
