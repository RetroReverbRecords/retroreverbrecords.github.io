How it works

Your music, released on a real label. It stays yours, and you keep everything it earns.

Physical merch

Cassettes, CDs, vinyl, MiniDiscs and shirts from RRR and its artists. Stock and prices are live on Bandcamp and change as runs sell out.

For artists

Sell your merch with us

List your shirts, cassettes, CDs, vinyl and prints on your own Bandcamp page, linked to RRR, and in front of the whole community. Fans pay you directly. RRR charges a flat €2.00 per item listed and takes no share of your sales. Send us photos and details and we can set the listing up for you.

RRR doesn't handle shipping, refunds or customer questions: you post the items yourself or use a print-on-demand or fulfilment company that makes and ships them for you.

What you need to list merch
  • Rights: you own or have permission for every design, photo and logo. No band, film or brand artwork you don't own.
  • Photos: at least one clear photo, 1200px or bigger. More angles sell better.
  • Details: title, description, price, sizes and colours, and how many you have.
  • Shipping: the country it ships from and postage costs for your country, Europe and the rest of the world. Weigh a packed item at the post office first so your postage is right.
  • Posting on time: post within the time you promise and send proof of postage, with tracking where possible. Bandcamp doesn't make, store or ship anything.
  • Problems and returns: you're the seller, so you answer fans' questions and replace or refund anything that arrives faulty or gets lost.
  • Print on demand: Bandcamp doesn't connect to Printful and similar companies, so each order has to be placed with them by hand.

Bandcamp keeps 10% of merch sales plus payment fees. RRR takes no share: just the €2.00 listing fee.
