How it works

RRR membership is non-exclusive, and every release stays 100% yours: your rights, your money. RRR releases stay in the catalogue for at least 18 months, with RRR as the only label for the first 12. Direct payment

Bandcamp pays you, not us

Bandcamp customer→Your Bandcamp release→Paid to you directly

Your Bandcamp account must be linked to RetroReverbRecords before an RRR release can be approved. This allows eligible RRR releases to remain connected to the label while Bandcamp payments are made directly to you. RRR never collects your Bandcamp sales.

  1. Be an active artist member

    Join as an artist on the membership page.

  2. Link your Bandcamp to the RRR label

    Two ways: password linking (recommended, full promotion and stats) or a Bandcamp invite. Compare them. Linking doesn't make you exclusive, and it gets you free Bandcamp VIP membership.

  3. Submit a specific release

    Tell us which release, and why it fits an RRR series.

  4. RRR reviews it

    We approve it into a series or let you know it wasn't selected this time.

  5. Only that release gets RRR status

    It gets a series badge and a catalogue number, like RRSYN-001. The rest of your music is unaffected.

Linking your Bandcamp

What linking means, and what it doesn't

What linking does

  • Your RRR releases appear on your Bandcamp page and the RRR label page, in front of RRR's followers.
  • Bandcamp pays you directly. RRR never touches your money.
  • You get free Bandcamp VIP.
  • With password linking, RRR can also use Bandcamp's promotion tools for your RRR releases: promo codes, features and page help.

What it doesn't change

  • Only your RRR releases are affected. All your other releases stay exactly as they are.
  • Keep releasing on your own, with collaborators and with other labels. Bandcamp lets an artist link to any number of labels.
  • Your payment settings stay yours. RRR never views or changes them.
  • You can unlink any time: just ask.

How password linking works

  1. Ask below. We email you a placeholder password made just for you.
  2. On Bandcamp, set your password to the placeholder (Settings → Account), then press "Done, I've set it" in the email.
  3. We link your page, usually within a few hours, and email you "Linked ✓".
  4. Change your password to a new private one straight away. Your page stays linked.

RRR will never ask for your real password, by email, message or on this website.

Robot checks. Every release gets an automatic sound check and an AI check. The AI check only raises a flag: a person listens, or you tell us about AI use. Nobody is turned down by a robot.
