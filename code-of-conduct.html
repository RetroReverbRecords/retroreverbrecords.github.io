Code of conduct

How we treat each other in the RRR Community: in group chats, comments, lives and releases. Every member agrees to this when they sign up.

A

Our values

Freedom of expression with respect for every creed, gender, culture, faith, sexual orientation, race and nationality.

Respectful collaboration. We work with each other, not against each other.

Helping artists grow, creatively and as people.

Supporting communities and local scenes.

Transparency in everything we do.

Enjoying it. We do this because we love it.

We do

  • Support other members' releases, not only our own
  • Leave real comments and give honest, kind feedback
  • Credit everyone who worked on a track
  • Declare AI use honestly
  • Tell RRR early about delays or problems
  • Keep other members' personal details and group chats private
  • Raise disagreements calmly, by email to RRR

We don't

  • Harass, bully, threaten or discriminate against anyone
  • Use bots, paid streams, loops, fake accounts or any fake engagement
  • Spam group chats or members with self-promotion
  • Upload music, samples or artwork we don't have the rights to
  • Share other people's private messages or details
  • Bring the community into disrepute in public
B

If something goes wrong

  1. Tell us

    Email retroreverbrecords@gmail.com . We treat reports in confidence.

  2. We look into it

    We listen to everyone involved before deciding anything.

  3. We act fairly

    Depending on how serious it is: a friendly word, a formal warning, points removed, removal from groups, suspension, or ending the membership.
