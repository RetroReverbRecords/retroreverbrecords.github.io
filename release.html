Why artists release with RRR

Most labels ask for a demo, then you wait for months. RRR works differently: we're a community label. You join, pick a release date in the calendar, send your files, and your music comes out on Bandcamp and all the streaming services (Spotify, Apple Music and the rest) under the Retro Reverb Records name.

  • You keep 100% of your royalties and all your rights. RRR takes nothing.
  • Non-exclusive. Release independently or with other labels whenever you like.
  • Bandcamp and streaming together for one small upload fee.
  • Promotion from the community: posts on RRR socials, the RRR YouTube channel, plays on The Bandcamp Hour, and a network of radio shows, playlists and blogs.
  • Your own dashboard: releases, bookings, sales, stats, points and belts in one place.
  • A real community: members support each other's releases, and fans collect your Synth Stars card.

What it costs

  • Artist membership: €2.00 a month, cancel any time.
  • Upload fee per release (one fee covers Bandcamp, streaming or both): single €2.00, EP €2.50, album €3.00.
  • Paid by PayPal (cards accepted). Full details in the membership terms.
5

Our AI policy

We release music made by people. We don't accept fully AI-generated tracks. AI may play a supporting role if you declare it, but undeclared AI is refused. Details in the release policy.

6

Common questions

Do you accept demos?
The quickest way to release with RRR is to join and book a date. If you want to be hand-picked, members can put a release forward for an RRR series.

Do I keep my rights?
Yes. You keep full ownership of your music and 100% of your royalties.

Is it exclusive?
No. Only the releases you choose to put out with RRR are RRR releases. For streaming, RRR is the only distributor of that exact recording for 18 months, because a second upload gets removed. Bandcamp and YouTube are never exclusive.

How fast can I release?
You choose the date. Send your files at least 3 weeks before release for streaming and 2 weeks before for Bandcamp.

Who's behind RRR?
An independent label and artist community running since 2018, home of The Bandcamp Hour. Read our story.
