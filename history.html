Since 2016

RRR history

Retro Reverb Records is an independent label for synthwave, electronic and alternative music, founded in 2016 by Colin Bouchard and musician Alex Vecchietti. Charity compilations, physical releases, live and online events, radio and podcasts, with artists from around the world.

  1. 2016

    The label is founded

    Colin Bouchard and Alex Vecchietti start Retro Reverb Records, building a roster of synthwave and electronic artists including Alex Vecchietti, The Subtheory, Honey Beard, Dark Smoke Signal and Eden Future.

    About – Work With Us

  2. 2019

    RetroReverbRhythms Vol. 1

    A multi-artist charity compilation raising funds for Cancer Research UK and Michiraggiodisole, with Timecop1983, NINA, Sunglasses Kid, Honey Beard, The Subtheory and Dark Smoke Signal. Promoted by The State of Synth and Nightride FM around an RRR charity event in London.

    Bandcamp · The State of Synth S1E13

  3. 2020

    Flashback, MiniDisc Day and Beirut Relief Rhythms

    On 9 May, RRR and TimeSlave Recordings present Flashback, an international online music event with Forever Synth and Nightride FM. RRR takes part in International MiniDisc Day with a compilation. In August, the label supports Beirut Relief Rhythms, organised by Andy Hill of The Subtheory after the Beirut explosion, with The Subtheory, Alex Vecchietti, Cybertronix, Dark Smoke Signal, Eden Future and more.

    International MiniDisc Day · Beirut Relief Rhythms on Bandcamp · Dark Smoke Signal interview

  4. 2021

    Inside The Mix and a second MiniDisc Day

    The podcast Inside The Mix devotes an episode to how RRR runs as an independent label: artist relationships and artist development. RRR releases another MiniDisc compilation for International MiniDisc Day.

    Inside The Mix · International MiniDisc Day

  5. 2022

    RetroReverbRhythms Vol. 2

    A second charity compilation, this time for the Mental Health Foundation, covered by The State of Synth and launched with a dedicated Nightride FM album-launch show.

    Bandcamp · Nightride FM launch party

  6. On air

    Radio, podcasts and The Bandcamp Hour

    Nightride FM's Kaarin's Podcast dedicates an episode to RRR with Bouchard, Vecchietti and Lloyd. The label runs The Bandcamp Hour on Spectrum On Air.

    Kaarin's EP94 · Spectrum On Air

  7. Now

    The RRR Community

    RRR becomes a community-first label: membership for artists and fans, releases where artists keep 100% of their royalties, and Synth Stars collectable cards. See membership
