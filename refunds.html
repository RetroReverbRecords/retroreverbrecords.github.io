Refund policy

When you can get your money back, and how. Part of the RRR Member Agreement.

Draft Beta version for review before launch.

Withdraw from contract hereAsk for a refund

1. Your 14 days to change your mind

  • If you live in the EU or UK and join as a consumer, you can withdraw from your membership within 14 days of joining, without giving a reason. Use the withdrawal page or email us.
  • We refund everything you paid for the membership within 14 days of your withdrawal, to the same payment method (PayPal).
  • Upload fees and YouTube fees: you can also withdraw a booking within 14 days. But if you ask us to start work straight away (you tick a box when you book) and we've already uploaded your release or video, you can't withdraw that booking any more. If we've started but not finished, we refund the part we haven't done.

2. Membership after the first 14 days

  • Cancel any time, in PayPal or by email. No more payments are taken.
  • Your membership runs to the end of the month you've paid for. We don't refund part-months.
  • Pause (Supporter Status) fees work the same way.

3. Upload and YouTube fees

  • Full refund if you cancel before we start uploading, or if RRR can't release your music or video.
  • If your assets arrive late and the date moves, your fee moves with it. You can also ask for a refund instead.
  • No refund once your release or video has been uploaded, because the work is done.
  • No refund if Bandcamp removes a release because of AI use you didn't declare. AI-generated music can only be released through streaming.

4. Printed cards and merch

  • Printed Synth Stars cards: you can return unopened cards within 14 days of receiving them for a refund of the card price. Cards that arrive damaged or get lost are replaced or refunded. Postage for returns is paid by you unless the card was faulty.
  • Merch is sold by the artist from their own Bandcamp page, so returns and refunds are handled by the artist, under the returns terms on each item. The €2.00 merch listing fee is refunded if RRR hasn't set up the listing yet.

5. Mistakes and other cases

  • Charged twice or charged by mistake? We refund the extra payment in full.
  • Removed for breaking the rules: we don't refund membership already used. We refund upload fees for releases or videos we haven't uploaded yet.
  • RRR closes a service: we refund anything you've paid for and not received.

6. Model withdrawal form

You don't have to use this form. The withdrawal page or any clear email works too.

To Retro Reverb Records, retroreverbrecords@gmail.com: I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the supply of the following service: ______. Ordered on / received on: ______. Name: ______. Address: ______. Signature (only if this form is sent on paper): ______. Date: ______.

7. How to ask

  • To withdraw in your first 14 days, use Withdraw from contract here. You get an email confirming we've received it.
  • For anything else, email retroreverbrecords@gmail.com with your member ID and what the payment was for. We reply within 7 days.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 14 days of us agreeing.
  • Please talk to us before opening a PayPal dispute. We'd rather sort it out quickly.
  • This policy doesn't affect your legal rights as a consumer.
