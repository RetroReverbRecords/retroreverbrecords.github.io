Giveaway rules (for the record)

  • Prize: 6 months of free Retro Reverb Records membership (artist or fan, your choice). No cash alternative.
  • Who could enter: anyone aged 18 or over. One entry per person: extra form entries with the same email, and repeat chat messages from the same username, were ignored.
  • How to enter (free, no purchase needed), either: the form on this page, or during The Bandcamp Hour on Sunday 8 November typing Welcome to the family – (your name) in the live chat on the RRR YouTube stream or on Mixcloud (Spectrum On Air).
  • Closing time: Sunday 8 November 2026 at 17:45 Italy time (16:45 UK). Extended from 25 October and 1 November; entries already received still counted.
  • How the winner was picked: one entry chosen at random on a live online wheel during The Bandcamp Hour on Sunday 8 November.
