Request a refund

Check below whether your payment can be refunded, then tell us what happened. The full rules are in the Refund Policy.

✅ You can claim a refund if

  • You were charged twice or by mistake.
  • You cancel a release or YouTube booking before we start uploading.
  • RRR can't release your music or video.
  • Your assets arrived late, the date moved, and you'd rather have a refund than a new date.
  • A curator didn't answer your RRR Submit track within 14 days.
  • Printed cards arrived damaged or got lost, or you return them unopened within 14 days.
  • RRR closes a service you've paid for and not received.
  • The €2 merch listing fee, if we haven't set up the listing yet.

Changed your mind within 14 days of joining or booking? Use Withdraw from contract here instead. It's the quickest way.

❌ These can't be refunded

  • Part-months of membership after the first 14 days. When you cancel, your membership runs to the end of the month you've paid for and no more payments are taken.
  • A release or video that has already been uploaded, because the work is done.
  • A release Bandcamp removes because of AI use you didn't declare.
  • Membership already used if you were removed for breaking the rules (we still refund uploads we haven't done).
  • Merch you bought from an artist: the artist handles returns from their own Bandcamp page.

We reply within 7 days. Agreed refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 14 days. Please talk to us before opening a PayPal dispute: we'd rather sort it out quickly. This doesn't affect your legal rights as a consumer.
