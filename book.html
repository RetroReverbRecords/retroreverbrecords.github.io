Uploading it yourself: the steps
  1. Make sure your Bandcamp is linked to the RRR label.
  2. On your Bandcamp page: + add → album (or track). Upload lossless audio (WAV, AIFF or FLAC) and artwork at least 1400 × 1400 px.
  3. Set it up as a pre-order with your booked release date, so it goes live on the right day by itself. Don't publish it early as a normal release.
  4. In the album's credits, paste exactly: Released by RetroReverbRecords · ℗ & © 2026 your artist name (you keep the copyright: the © line names you, the release line names RRR)
  5. Add your genre as the first tag, plus "retro reverb records".
  6. On release day, send a Bandcamp message to your fans.

Our robot checks your Bandcamp page (date, credit line, tags) and tells us only if something's missing.

RRR uploading it for you: what we need
  • Audio: WAV, AIFF or FLAC, 16 or 24-bit, one file per track, named "01 Track title".
  • Artwork: square, at least 1400 × 1400 px (3000 × 3000 is best), JPG or PNG, no blur, no web addresses or logos you don't own.
  • Words: release title, track titles in order, a short description, credits (who wrote, played, produced, mixed, mastered), lyrics if you want them shown.
  • Price: your price for the release (and per track if different), and whether fans can pay more.
  • Tags: up to 5 genre or mood tags.
  • Put it all in one Google Drive or Dropbox folder and paste the link below, at least 2 weeks before release.
AI use in this release · required What counts?

Bandcamp bans music made "wholly or in substantial part" by AI. Heavily AI-generated releases can go to streaming only, not Bandcamp. Undeclared AI is at your own risk: if Bandcamp removes a release, RRR doesn't refund it.

Sell your merch with us

Your merch goes on your own Bandcamp page, linked to RRR, so it shows on the RRR page too and fans pay you directly. RRR charges a flat €2.00 per item listed and takes no share of your sales. Send us the photos and details and we can set the listing up for you. You post the orders and handle refunds and fans' questions, yourself or with a print-on-demand or fulfilment company (suggestions).

Merch listing guide: send everything ready to copy

If RRR sets up your listing, we copy exactly what you send. We don't write descriptions or set prices for you, so please send it finished.

  • Title: "What it is · Release or design name", e.g. Cassette · Neon Born (limited 50).
  • Description (3–5 short lines): what it is, material or format, sizes, what's included (e.g. digital download), edition size, and your posting time.
  • Price: cover your making cost + postage materials + Bandcamp's 10% + about 4% payment fees, then add your profit. Example: a print-on-demand shirt that costs you €13 → sell at €22–25.
  • Postage: weigh one packed item at the post office, then set a price for your country, Europe and the rest of the world.
  • Photos: at least one, 1200 px or bigger, plain background. Best: front, back, a close-up and one being worn or used.
  • Sizes, colours and stock: list each option and how many you have. Print on demand = unlimited.
  • Returns line for the listing, e.g. "Faulty or lost items replaced or refunded. Contact me within 14 days."
