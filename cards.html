Collect all 52

Illustrated collector cards of RRR artists, released in seasons of 52. Every artist member can claim their own card for free: we create a one-of-a-kind card of you within 4 weeks and add it to the current season. Printed copies to collect, swap and trade are coming soon.

A

Claim your card

Free for artist members. Send us a photo and a few details; we design your card, show it to you first, and add it to the season within 4 weeks.

What goes on your card

  • Name and style (Eden Future · Shredwave)
  • Your likeness in a neon 80s scene with your gear
  • 6 stat bars: Vocal Power, Instrument Skill, Production IQ, Live Voltage, Cult Following and your own fun stat
  • Interesting fact about you
  • Card number in the season, and the RRR Certified seal
B

Check a card

Every official card has a serial number on the front and a check code under the holographic sticker on the back. Enter both to see if a card is genuine.

How to spot an official card

  • Serial printed on the front, e.g. S1-01-P-0007: season, card, edition, copy.
  • Holographic RRR sticker on the back, hiding the check code. A peeled or missing sticker is a warning sign.
  • The serial and code match on this page.
  • Edition letters: M member card, P numbered print, A artist-signed single edition.
C

Swapping and selling

You can swap and sell your cards with other collectors. The check code goes with the card, so the new owner can check it too.

Buying from someone else? Ask for the serial and check the card here before you pay.

Photos and scans are fine for showing off, but only printed cards with a registered serial are official.

Found a fake? Email us a photo of both sides at retroreverbrecords@gmail.com.
