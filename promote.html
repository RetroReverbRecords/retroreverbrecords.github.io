Common questions

How much does it cost to promote my music with RRR?

Artist membership is €2.00 a month and includes 2 free promo posts a month on the RRR socials. Uploads to the RRR YouTube channel are €1.00 per video, and RRR Submit costs from €1.50 per curator.

How do I get my track played on The Bandcamp Hour?

Send it through the RetroReverbRecords page on Groover: you're guaranteed a listen and written feedback. Groover charges per submission and shows the price before you pay. The show airs every Sunday, 4 to 6pm Italy time (3 to 5pm UK), on Spectrum On Air, YouTube, Facebook and XPTV3. About the show

Are the plays and followers real?

Yes. No bots, no fake streams. Promotion comes from real artists and fans in the community, and members earn points for supporting each other's releases.

How far ahead do I book a social post?

At least 3 days ahead. We check it and it goes out automatically. Book a post

What if a curator doesn't answer?

Every RRR Submit curator listens properly and writes honest feedback within 14 days, or you get your money back.
