Before you book

  • You need an active Artist Membership. Supporter Status pauses booking.
  • Bandcamp assets: at least 3 weeks before release.
  • Streaming assets: at least 2 weeks before release.
  • Bandcamp editorial is not an RRR service. If you want to try for it, submit yourself at least 8–9 weeks before release.
  • Assets go in your designated Google Drive folder.
