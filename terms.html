1. About these terms

These terms apply to everyone who joins the RRR Community as an artist or a fan. By ticking the agreement boxes and typing your name when you sign up, you agree to them. For artists they also include the AI-Generated Music Policy, the Release Policy and the Code of Conduct.

These terms replace RRR's old label contracts for new members. If you signed an earlier Agreement of Collaboration with RRR, that agreement still covers the releases made under it until you and RRR agree otherwise in writing.

2. Membership and fees

  • Artist membership: €2.00 a month. Fan membership: €1.00 a month.
  • Community Supporter Status (a break): €1.00 a month for artists, €0.50 for fans. You keep your membership, rank, points and achievements, but can't book releases, promotions or artist services until you reactivate.
  • Release upload fees, per release, one fee covering Bandcamp, streaming or both: single €2.00, EP of 2–5 tracks €2.50, album of 6+ tracks €3.00. YouTube upload: €1.00 per video. Merch listing: €2.00 per item, one-off. Merch is sold from your own Bandcamp page and paid to you directly; you handle posting, refunds and customer questions.
  • All prices are in euros. Payments go through PayPal. Membership renews monthly until you cancel.
  • Cancelling: cancel at any time in PayPal or by emailing us. Your membership runs to the end of the month you've paid for.
  • 14 days to change your mind: consumers can withdraw within 14 days of joining or booking, using Withdraw from contract here. Refunds are explained in the Refund Policy.
  • Upload fees are paid when you book. If RRR can't release your music, we refund that fee.
  • We may change prices. We'll tell members by email at least 30 days before a change affects them, and you can cancel before it does.

3. Your music and your money

  • You own your music. You keep full copyright in your compositions, recordings, artwork and name.
  • Membership is non-exclusive. Becoming an RRR Member Artist, or linking your Bandcamp to RRR, doesn't make you exclusive to RRR or give RRR any of your music. You can release independently, work with other labels and sell from your own Bandcamp page. Only releases you submit to RRR and that RRR approves as an RRR Genre Release (or an RRR Signed Release under a separate signed agreement) are RRR releases. Everything else stays outside the RRR programme. Each RRR release has its own release window and catalogue period, explained below.
  • Bandcamp link for RRR releases. To have a release approved as an RRR release, your Bandcamp account must be linked to the RRR Bandcamp label account, so the release stays connected to RRR while Bandcamp pays you directly. RRR never collects your Bandcamp sales. Linked artists get free Bandcamp VIP membership.
  • RRR Bandcamp VIP subscribers. RRR reserves the right to offer RRR releases, or extras connected to them, to subscribers of the RRR Bandcamp VIP subscription early (for example, a few days before the public release date) to build interest. VIP subscription payments are RRR's and are not shared with artists. Sales of your release still pay you directly.
  • Two ways to link. Invite: RRR can only add releases to your page. Password linking: Bandcamp gives RRR full access to your artist account. If you choose password linking, RRR will only use that access to add and promote your RRR releases, set up promo links and codes, help with your page when you ask, and read your stats for your dashboard. RRR will never view or change your payment settings, delete or edit your other releases, or message your fans without your permission. RRR emails you a one-off placeholder password to set on Bandcamp while we link your page; change your password straight after. RRR never asks for your real password. You can ask RRR to remove the link at any time.
  • You keep 100% of your royalties from Bandcamp and streaming releases made through RRR. Bandcamp pays you directly. Streaming royalties are paid out automatically, about every 45 days. For Bandcamp this works by linking your Bandcamp account to the RRR label, so buyers pay you directly. Without the link we can't release on Bandcamp for you.
  • What you allow RRR to do: while your release is in the RRR catalogue, you give RRR a non-exclusive licence to host it on the RRR Bandcamp catalogue, distribute it through the RRR RouteNote account, upload videos you send us to the RRR YouTube channel, and use your name, image, artwork and music to promote it.
  • 12-month release window. For 12 months from release day, RRR is the only label or distributor releasing that recording. You can still play it live, share it on your socials and use it in your own videos. After 12 months you can also release or license it anywhere else. It only covers those exact recordings: remixes, extended mixes, edits, new versions, live versions and instrumentals are yours to release anywhere, any time. This avoids duplicate uploads, which streaming services flag and remove.
  • 18 months in the catalogue. Each RRR release stays in the RRR catalogue for at least 18 months from release day. After that it stays for as long as you like: it costs you nothing, and every sale and stream is still 100% yours. To take it down after 18 months, email us and we remove it within 30 days. Earlier removal only if there's a legal problem (for example a rights claim) or RRR agrees.
  • Compilations are permanent. If you agree to a track appearing on an RRR compilation, it stays on that compilation permanently. We always ask you first.
  • Promotion already made (posts, videos, newsletters, playlists) can stay up after a release is taken down. We just don't make new promotion for it.

4. Booking and delivering releases

  • Book every release through the RRR release calendar (the Book page). A release date is confirmed only when you accept RRR's calendar invite.
  • Deliver everything in the Release Policy to your RRR Google Drive folder at least 3 weeks before release for streaming and 2 weeks before for Bandcamp.
  • If assets arrive late, the release may move to the next free date.
  • Editorial pitching to Bandcamp and Spotify is done by you, not RRR. Our editorial guides show you how. You can add RRR to your Spotify for Artists team as your label and we'll help you manage it.

5. Promotion and offers

  • RRR may include releases in the catalogue in discount codes, giveaways and promotional offers.
  • RRR may share your release on its social media, playlists, newsletter, radio shows and website.
  • Promotion bookings (social posts, YouTube uploads) depend on available slots. RRR confirms each booking by email.

6. Your promises

When you send RRR music, artwork or video, you promise that:

  • you own it or have permission to release it, including any samples, beats, vocals or artwork from other people;
  • everyone who worked on it (featured artists, producers, remixers, co-writers) has agreed to the release and to how any money is shared between you; RRR is not responsible for splits between you and them;
  • it doesn't break anyone's copyright or other rights, and you've declared any AI use honestly;
  • the details you give us (names, credits, ISRC/EAN codes, metadata) are correct.

If a claim is made against RRR because one of these promises wasn't true, RRR may take the release down, and you agree to cover RRR's reasonable costs of dealing with it.

7. AI-Generated Music Policy

Bandcamp rule (January 2026): Bandcamp doesn't allow music made wholly or in substantial part by AI and removes releases it suspects. RRR therefore releases AI-generated music on streaming only. Every submission gets an automatic AI check; a flag means a person listens or you declare AI use. It never rejects on its own. Undeclared AI use is at your own risk: if Bandcamp removes your release, the upload fee isn't refunded.

RRR values artistic integrity and transparency. Artists must disclose how much AI was used in each release when they book it, covering composition, lyrics, instrumental or vocal synthesis, mixing and mastering, and sound design or sample generation.

No AI usageCreated entirely by human effort.
Partial AI assistanceAI helped with elements like sound design, lyric ideas or mixing; the core artistic work is human.
Heavily AI-generatedMost of the track, such as composition, lyrics or vocals, was made with AI.

Acceptable uses, as long as you remain the main creative force and declare them: AI mastering and mixing assistance; ideas (chords, rhythms, melodies) that you significantly develop; AI synths, samplers and plugins whose sounds you edit and layer; workflow and technical tools; lyric support where the final lyrics are yours; and visual or marketing support, as long as the material is original, cleared for commercial use and fits RRR's branding.

Anything beyond that, or where AI material forms most of a work without substantial human change, must be declared as heavily AI-generated. You're responsible for making sure AI material doesn't infringe anyone's copyright.

Inaccurate disclosure may lead to the song being removed from RRR platforms, your membership ending for repeated breaches, and legal review where copyright is infringed. This policy will be reviewed as the industry changes.

  • Points, belts and achievements have no cash value, can't be transferred, and may be adjusted if they were earned unfairly. RRR may change the points system and will announce changes.
