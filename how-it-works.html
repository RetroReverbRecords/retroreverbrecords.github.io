How it works

From sign-up to release day

RRR is a community label. You pay a small monthly membership, book releases for a one-off upload fee, and keep 100% of your royalties.

A

For artists

  1. Create your account

    Join as an artist for €2.00 a month. You get community membership, promotion opportunities and your own digital Synth Stars card, with the option to order a printed card plus postage. Create account

  2. Link your Bandcamp and Spotify

    Link your Bandcamp account to the RRR label so sales go straight to you: that is how you keep 100%. Choose password linking (recommended: full promotion and stats) or a Bandcamp invite. When your Bandcamp is linked to the RRR Bandcamp, you get free Bandcamp VIP membership for as long as your releases stay active in the catalogue. (Bandcamp VIP is separate from membership of this platform.) You can also add RRR to your Spotify for Artists team as your label so we can help manage it.

  3. Book your release

    Pick a release date in the RRR release calendar (dates are subject to availability) and choose Bandcamp, streaming or both. Upload fees: single €2.00, EP (2–5 tracks) €2.50, album €3.00. One fee covers Bandcamp, streaming or both. Book a release

  4. Send your assets on time

    Put your audio, artwork and release details in your designated Google Drive folder at least 3 weeks before release for streaming and at least 2 weeks before for Bandcamp. See the release policy for exactly what to send.

  5. Release day

    Your release goes live on your Bandcamp page and the RRR page, and on streaming through the RRR RouteNote account. Bandcamp payments go straight to your own account, and streaming royalties are paid out automatically about every 45 days. Both are automated.

  6. Pitch to editors yourself

    Bandcamp and Spotify editorial pitching is done by you, not RRR. Follow the editorial guides: Bandcamp 8–9 weeks ahead, Spotify at least 7 days ahead.

  7. Promote it with the community

    Book a promotional post on RRR social media, upload a video to the RRR YouTube channel, and submit to The Bandcamp Hour through Groover. See booking options

B

For fans

  1. Join as a fan

    Fan membership is €1.00 a month and lets you collect your favourite artists' Synth Stars cards, digital or printed plus postage. Create account

  2. Get close to the artists

    Talk directly with RRR artists and take part in community events and activities.

  3. Support and earn

    Buy music, share releases and help other members to earn community points and a shot at Fan of the Month.

Common questions

Is RRR exclusive?

No. RRR membership is non-exclusive. Release independently when you want, work with other labels when you want, and release selected projects through RRR when it makes sense. Only releases you choose to submit, and that RRR approves, become RRR releases. About Selected Releases

How long does a release stay in the RRR catalogue?

At least 18 months from release day, then for as long as you like. Most artists keep their releases up for good: it costs nothing and every sale and stream is still 100% yours. For the first 12 months RRR is the only label or distributor releasing those exact recordings. Remixes, extended mixes, edits and new versions are yours to release anywhere, any time. After 12 months you can release the originals anywhere too. Tracks on RRR compilations stay there permanently, and we always ask before adding one. Full terms

Do I keep my royalties?

Yes. You keep 100% of your royalties from Bandcamp and streaming releases made through RRR (Bandcamp still takes its usual sales fee), and you keep ownership of your music and intellectual property.

What if I need a break?

Switch to Community Supporter Status: €1.00 a month for artists, €0.50 for fans. You keep your membership, rank, points and achievements, but can't book releases or promotions until you reactivate. You can reactivate at any time.

Can RRR run offers on my release?

Yes. Releases in the RRR Bandcamp catalogue can be included in RRR discount codes, giveaways and promotional offers. This is a condition of the Bandcamp service.

How do I pay?

Everything is paid by PayPal, which also takes debit and credit cards. Membership is a monthly PayPal subscription you set up right after signing up. One-off fees, like release and YouTube uploads, are paid after you book. Payment opens when sign-up launches.

Where do I see my achievements?

Every member gets a member ID when they sign up. It opens your member dashboard: rank, points, achievements, releases, social posts and stats.
