RRR Member Agreement

The contract you sign when you join. Short and plain on purpose. The full detail sits in the Membership Terms, Code of Conduct, Refund Policy and Privacy Policy, which are part of this agreement.

Draft Beta version. RRR's full legal name, address and the governing law are added before launch, and a lawyer reviews it.

Join and sign

Between

Retro Reverb Records ("RRR") and you, the person who signs below as an artist member or a fan member.

Everyone agrees

  1. Membership. You join the RRR Community for a monthly fee (artists €2.00, fans €1.00), paid through PayPal. It renews each month until you cancel.
  2. Cancelling. Cancel any time in PayPal or by email. Your membership runs to the end of the month you've paid for. See the Refund Policy, including your 14 days to change your mind.
  3. Pausing. Take a break for €1.00 a month (artists) or €0.50 (fans). You keep your belt, points and achievements.
  4. Price changes. We tell you at least 30 days before a price change affects you. You can cancel before it does.
  5. Behaviour. You follow the Code of Conduct. If you don't, RRR may warn, suspend or remove you as set out there.
  6. Points and cards. Points, belts and digital cards have no cash value and can't be transferred. Points earned unfairly can be removed.
  7. Your data. We use your details only as the Privacy Policy says. We never sell them.
  8. Leaving. You can leave at any time. RRR can end your membership if you seriously or repeatedly break this agreement.
  9. Your rights. Nothing here takes away your legal rights as a consumer where you live.

Artists also agree

  1. Your music stays yours. You keep all rights in your music, artwork and name, and 100% of your royalties. RRR takes nothing from them.
  2. Non-exclusive membership. You can release independently and with other labels. Only releases you book with RRR, or submit and RRR approves, become RRR releases, each with the release window and catalogue period below.
  3. Permission to release. For each release you book, you let RRR host it on the RRR Bandcamp catalogue, distribute it to streaming services and promote it, including in discount codes, giveaways and offers. Each release stays in the RRR catalogue for at least 18 months from release day, then for as long as you like (takedown within 30 days of asking). For the first 12 months RRR is the only label or distributor releasing those exact recordings (remixes, extended mixes, edits and new versions are free to release anywhere); after that you can release them anywhere else too. Tracks you agree to put on an RRR compilation stay on it permanently. RRR may also offer it to RRR Bandcamp VIP subscribers early to build interest; VIP subscription money stays with RRR.
  4. Bandcamp link. RRR releases need your Bandcamp linked to the RRR label, by invite or password linking. Bandcamp pays you directly. With password linking, RRR only uses access for what the Terms allow and never touches your payment settings.
  5. What you promise. You own or have permission for everything you send us, everyone on the release has agreed, you credit collaborators, and you declare AI use honestly (AI policy).
  6. Deadlines. You send your assets on time: at least 2 weeks before release for Bandcamp and 3 weeks for streaming. Late assets can move your release date.
  7. Fees. Upload fees: single €2.00, EP €2.50, album €3.00, one fee for Bandcamp, streaming or both. YouTube upload €1.00. Merch listing €2.00 per item: merch is sold from your own Bandcamp page and you handle posting and refunds.

Signing

You sign this agreement on the sign-up form by ticking the box and typing your full legal name. RRR records your name, the date and time, and this version number, and emails you a copy. Your typed name has the same effect as a handwritten signature.
