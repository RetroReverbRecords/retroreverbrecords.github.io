A label for the people

Code of conduct

How we treat each other in the RRR Community: in group chats, comments, lives and releases. Every member agrees to this when they sign up.

A

Our values

Freedom of expression with respect for every creed, gender, culture, faith, sexual orientation, race and nationality.

Respectful collaboration. We work with each other, not against each other.

Helping artists grow, creatively and as people.

Supporting communities and local scenes.

Transparency in everything we do.

Enjoying it. We do this because we love it.

We do

  • Support other members' releases, not only our own
  • Leave real comments and give honest, kind feedback
  • Credit everyone who worked on a track
  • Declare AI use honestly
  • Tell RRR early about delays or problems
  • Keep other members' personal details and group chats private
  • Raise disagreements calmly, by email to RRR

We don't

  • Harass, bully, threaten or discriminate against anyone
  • Use bots, paid streams, loops, fake accounts or any fake engagement
  • Spam group chats or members with self-promotion
  • Upload music, samples or artwork we don't have the rights to
  • Share other people's private messages or details
  • Bring the community into disrepute in public
B

Report a problem

  1. Tell us

    Email retroreverbrecords@gmail.com with what happened, who, where and when, and screenshots or links if you have them. Reports are confidential.

  2. Protect yourself straight away

    You can block or mute anyone yourself on Discord, Instagram and the other apps. You don't need to wait for us.

  3. We look into it

    We reply within 7 days, listen to everyone involved, and decide fairly. If someone is in danger, contact the police first.

C

Blocking and removing abusive users

What happens when someone breaks this Code. We choose the step that fits how serious it is and whether it has happened before.

1

A friendly word

For small, first-time problems. A private message from RRR. Nothing is recorded against you.

2

Formal warning

By email, saying what rule was broken. It stays on record for 12 months. Points gained unfairly are removed.

3

Suspension, up to 30 days

No bookings, claims, submissions or points, and you're muted in the community spaces. Your membership fee is paused while you're suspended.

4

Removal and block

Your membership ends, payments stop, your belt, points and profile are removed, and you're removed from the community spaces. Your email is blocked from joining again.

Straight to removal

Zero tolerance

  • Threats, or encouraging violence or self-harm
  • Hate speech or discrimination
  • Sexual harassment, or any sexual content involving minors (also reported to the police)
  • Sharing someone's private details (doxxing)
  • Payment fraud, fake payments or chargeback abuse
  • Releasing music you don't have the rights to, on purpose

What happens to your music and money

If you're removed

  • Your music stays yours and stays on your own Bandcamp page
  • RRR removes RRR branding, series listings and the RRR label link from your releases
  • Upload fees for releases we haven't uploaded yet are refunded (see the Refund Policy)
  • Membership already used isn't refunded

Fair process

You'll always know why, and you can appeal

  • For a warning, suspension or removal we email you what we decided, which rule, why, and how to appeal.
  • Appeal within 14 days by replying to that email. We look at it again and answer within 14 days. Where we can, someone not involved in the first decision reviews it.
  • In an emergency we can suspend first and explain straight after.
  • We keep records of decisions for 12 months (removals: as long as the block lasts), only to run this policy.

Monthly newsletter

New releases, top posts, what's next

One email on the 1st of every month. Unsubscribe any time.
